Desktop Island Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

Desktop Island is a Windows desktop overlay developed by Orhan Doğan Kaplan. This policy explains what information the app uses and when it is sent to other services.

Information used on your device

The app reads the currently active Windows media session to show track information and to provide playback controls. This information is processed on your PC and is not sent to the developer.

Focus timer values, notes, calendar notes, your optional local video path, and any YouTube API key or Spotify access token you enter are stored locally on your PC. The app does not send these local notes or timer data to the developer.

When you use music search or playback

When you request a YouTube search, the search text and your YouTube Data API key are sent to the YouTube Data API to return results. When you request a Spotify search, the search text and the Spotify access token you supplied are sent to Spotify's API. The app makes these requests only when you use the corresponding search feature.

If you choose to play an embedded YouTube video or Spotify track, the relevant service receives requests from its embedded player. Google/YouTube and Spotify may collect information under their own policies. Review the Google Privacy Policy and Spotify Privacy Policy.

Data sharing and analytics

The developer does not operate an account system, advertising network, analytics service, or telemetry server for Desktop Island. The app does not sell your information. Information is shared with YouTube or Spotify only as described above when you choose to use those services.

Deleting locally stored information

You can remove notes and calendar notes in the app. Music service credentials and the optional video path can be removed from the app's local configuration file at %LOCALAPPDATA%\DesktopIsland\config.json. Uninstalling the app may not remove all locally stored settings automatically.

Children

Desktop Island is a general-purpose desktop utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. YouTube and Spotify content and their services are subject to those providers' age requirements and policies.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated if the app's data practices change. For privacy questions, contact the developer through the developer's GitHub profile.
